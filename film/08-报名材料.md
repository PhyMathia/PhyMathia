## 一 · Logline（一句话）

**中文**
> 一根弦被拨响，你听见一个音；这个音其实是八个音同时响。有人把其中七个拿走，它还是一个音——只是不再是它了。

**English**
> A string is struck, and you hear one note. The note is actually eight notes sounding at once. Someone takes seven of them away. It is still a note — it is just no longer itself.

## 二 · Synopsis（剧情梗概，约 150 词）

**中文**

黑暗里，一根弦被绷直。它被拨响，先是一个纯粹的正弦，然后第二个、第三个……第八个分量依次出现。同一个音高，越来越多的东西。八个分量各挂着一个字母，拼成 D E E P S E E K。

第七个分量被拿走。声音几乎没有变化。剩下的七个字母仍然可读——一个缺了一个字母的词，仍然是一个词。

然后所有的泛音被一根根抽走。音高一点没变，声音却退回成一条完美的正弦：正确、干净、什么也没说。名字只剩下一个字母 D。

一条金线亮起，标出八个分量原来站过的位置。它们被一根根放回去。残差从 0.500 掉到 0.250、0.060、0.008，最后跳到 0.000——弦的形状在那一帧严丝合缝地变回原来的曲线。

八个分量升空，变成八个节点，连成第一张图。图铺成地面，长成一片大陆。每一座岛是一个基频，岛与岛之间的每条航线，都是两条泛音列上精确相等的那一个频率。

最后，镜头回到那根弦。基频被拿走，周期括号一格没动。音高还是那个音高。

**English**

In the dark, a string is pulled taut. It is struck: first a pure sine, then a second partial, a third… until eight are sounding at once. One pitch, more and more inside it. Each partial carries a letter; together they spell D E E P S E E K.

The seventh partial is taken away. The sound barely changes. Seven letters remain — a word missing one letter is still a word.

Then the overtones are removed one by one. The pitch does not move, but the sound retreats into a perfect sine: correct, clean, saying nothing. Only the letter D is left.

A gold line lights up along the base, marking where the eight partials stood. They are put back, one at a time. The residual falls from 0.500 to 0.250, 0.060, 0.008 — then to 0.000, and in that single frame the string's shape snaps back to the exact curve it started from.

The eight partials rise and become eight nodes, and the first graph appears. The graph unfolds into ground, and the ground grows into a continent. Every island is a fundamental; every route between two islands is a frequency the two harmonic series share exactly.

At the end, the camera returns to the string. The fundamental is removed. The bracket marking one period does not move. The pitch is still the pitch.

## 三 · Director's Statement（导演阐述）

**中文**

这部片子不是从一个人的故事开始的，是从一个问题开始的：**为什么一次总结，会让人觉得"我已经懂了"？**

一个音可以回答这个问题。你听见的那个音，其实是八个音同时响；把其中七个拿走，它还是"一个音"——音高一点没变，只是不再是它了。这件事不需要比喻。声音本来就是这样构成的，而我们对"懂了"的错觉，也是这样构成的。

我选一根弦，是因为它是最小的、同时能被看见和被听见的东西。它的每一部分都可以算出来，算出来的结果可以逐帧核对。片子里没有一个数字是装饰：角落的残差是真的残差，画面上的齿高是真的系数，你听到的声音和看到的曲线来自同一组数。

片子里没有人。没有脸，没有手，没有讲述者。**因为这不是一个关于谁的故事。** 弦被拨响，但拨它的东西永远在画外。观众不必同情任何人，只需要听。

这也是一部关于"还原"的片子，而不是关于"失去"的。失去很容易拍：把东西拿走就行了。难的是让观众相信它真的回得来——不是变清楚了，是一根一根放回去，误差降到零。那一下，是这部片子唯一想让人爽的地方。

最后一个镜头，我们把基频也拿走了。音高没有变。**名字可以拿走，它还在。**

**English**

This film does not begin with a person's story. It begins with a question: why does a summary make us feel that we have understood?

A single note can answer it. The note you hear is eight notes sounding at once. Take seven of them away and it is still "a note" — the pitch has not moved; it is simply no longer itself. No metaphor is required. Sound is built this way, and so is the illusion that we have understood.

I chose a string because it is the smallest thing that can be both seen and heard. Every part of it can be computed, and the computation can be checked frame by frame. Nothing on screen is decorative: the residual in the corner is the real residual, the height of each tooth is the real coefficient, and the sound you hear and the curve you see come from the same numbers.

There is no one in the film. No face, no hand, no narrator — because this is not a story about anyone. The string is struck, but whatever strikes it stays outside the frame. The audience is not asked to feel for a person. Only to listen.

This is also a film about restoration, not loss. Loss is easy: take something away. The hard part is making the audience believe it can actually come back — not that it becomes clearer, but that it is put back, piece by piece, until the error reaches zero. That moment is the only thrill this film is after.

In the last shot, we take the fundamental away too. The pitch does not change. The name can be taken away. It is still there.

## 四 · Technical Statement（技术陈述）

**中文**

全片 270 秒、6480 帧，全部由代码生成，没有一帧是拍摄的，也没有一帧是文生图。

- **画面**：弦的每一条曲线都是 `Σ aₙ sin(nπx/L)·cos(2πfₙt+φₙ)` 在当帧相位的取值。梳齿的高度就是 `aₙ`，齿的位置就是 `fₙ`。残差显示的是 `max|f − S_N|` 的真值。
- **声音**：与画面共用同一组 `aₙ` 做加法合成，不使用采样、不使用音色库。音频的频谱与画面上的梳齿可以被同一份数据核对（FFT）。
- **时间**：110 Hz 在 24 fps 下无法直接拍摄。画面以 1/220 的时间尺度呈现（110 Hz → 0.5 Hz 可见），声音是实时的。整部正片是真实时间里 1.23 秒的一声长音。
- **确定性**：渲染管线带 hash 闸门——同一帧重渲三次必须得到同一个 hash，否则不允许进入长渲。这一点已在前期实测中确认是必需的（透明粒子 + Bloom 会导致帧不可复现）。

**这不是"AI 生成的影像"。这是人写的代码，跑出来的结果。** 代码是作者，渲染是笔。

**English**

Two hundred and seventy seconds, 6,480 frames, all generated by code. Nothing is shot; nothing is text-to-image.

- **Image**: every curve of the string is the value of `Σ aₙ sin(nπx/L)·cos(2πfₙt+φₙ)` at that frame's phase. The height of each tooth is `aₙ`; its position is `fₙ`. The residual shown on screen is the true `max|f − S_N|`.
- **Sound**: additive synthesis from the same `aₙ`, with no samples and no instrument library. The spectrum of the audio and the comb on screen can be checked against the same data (FFT).
- **Time**: 110 Hz cannot be filmed at 24 fps. The image runs at a time scale of 1/220 (110 Hz appears as 0.5 Hz); the sound is real-time. The whole film is one 1.23-second note stretched to four and a half minutes.
- **Determinism**: the pipeline has a hash gate — the same frame rendered three times must produce the same hash, or it does not enter the long render. Early tests confirmed this is necessary (transparent particles plus bloom produce irreproducible frames).

**This is not "AI-generated imagery". It is code, written by a person, and run.** The code is the author; the render is the pen.
